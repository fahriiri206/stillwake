Stillwake

The lanterns of the moonlit archipelago have gone dark. One small mailboat, a handful of brass buoys, and a harbor that will not open until every light is aboard.

12 charts · no timer · a route you can repair

The ship's rules

Keep the lanterns burning.

1 Place buoys

Left-click open water to lay a brass buoy. Click it again to cycle its heading: east, south, west, north, then away. Right-click removes it. Lanterns, special water, the harbor, the mooring, land and reef take no buoy.

2 Plan the wake

The boat sails one cell at a time in its current heading. Entering a buoy's cell turns the bow to the buoy's heading. The dotted line is your preview.

3 Read the water

A current forces its own heading. A whirlpool turns the heading a quarter clockwise. Currents and whirlpools win over buoys, but buoys cannot be placed there.

4 Carry every light

Collect every gold lantern, then enter the harbor. The harbor refuses a boat that arrives early. Reefs, land and the chart's edge end the voyage; so does a closed loop.
